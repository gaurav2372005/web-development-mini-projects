01 — Origin

Built from compassion, run with discipline.

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh — and registered, certified, and accountable from day one.

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organisation, licensed by the Central Government, working at the intersection of education, food security, women's empowerment, animal welfare, environmental restoration, and youth employability.

What began as a small group of volunteers in Chhattisgarh has grown into a network spanning 28 states, with over 200 active volunteers and 30,000+ interns trained through our flagship skill-development programme, Project Vikas.

We believe transparency builds trust — which is why every certification, registration, and number on this page is verifiable.

  • ISO Certification9001 : 2015
  • Tax Exemption80G & 12A
  • Corporate GivingCSR-1 Registered
